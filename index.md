**Kilo Man Jar-O** (working title) is a game about Kilo, a tiny hero just 10 centimeters tall, crossing a giant garden toward a pickle jar the size of a mountain. It's a story about how being small never stops you from reaching something big. This is where the making of it gets shared, one small step at a time.
