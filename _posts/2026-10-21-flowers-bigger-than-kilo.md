**TL;DR:** The flower patch now shows up beside the trail in a real run, from about 45 m to 60 m. Next to a 10 cm hero, petals are roofs and stalks are trees.
{: .tldr}

Last time the flower patch floated or vanished when we stretched it. This time we went for a walk through it instead.

## Flowers beside the trail {% include status.html s="In progress" %}

You can now set how many flowers there are, how wide the patch is, and how big each flower grows. The same settings always give the same layout, so a patch stays put from run to run.

Here's the view from the trail at 45 metres in. The hero is the small grey figure at the bottom. The flowers beside him are taller than he is by a lot.

And at 60 metres, looking up from the middle of it all. Those pink things are petals:

A peek behind the scenes, as it is. Here's the patch from straight above in the editor, with all the outlines and arrows we use for moving things around:

{% include figure.html src="/assets/img/kilo-flowers-top-down.jpg" alt="The flower patch seen from straight above in the editor, a ring of pink petals around the trail, with orange outlines and move arrows." caption="Behind the scenes: the patch from above, outlines and all." width="1251" height="770" %}

It's still **in progress**, not working. We haven't confirmed that the patch stays on the ground when stretched, and we haven't play-tested it over a whole run yet.

## Still cooking

- **Keeping the patch on the ground** when it's stretched.
- **A play-test over a whole run**, to make sure it holds up.
- **A rounder, earthier trail**, and a part-by-part art pass on leaves, trunks, petals, roots and pebbles.
