Kilo Man Jar-O is openly built with AI help, and that's part of the story.

- **One designer.** I design and play-test the game, and I make the final call on everything.
- **Two AI helpers.** One works directly inside Unreal Engine, building Blueprints, materials and level pieces. The other plans each step, checks the work, keeps the project's history safe, and measures performance.
- **The crew has names.** Around here they answer to Cadbury (keeps the plan), Gilfoyle (engineering), Bachman (research) and Jared (checks everything twice).
- **A human checks it.** Nothing counts as "working" until I've played it, and nothing on this blog goes live until I approve it.

This note will be kept in line with the AI disclosures on the game's store page and crowdfunding campaign.
