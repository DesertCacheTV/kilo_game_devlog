Game-making has a lot of odd words. Here are the ones you'll see around this devlog, in plain language.

<dl class="glossary">
  <dt>Back burner</dt><dd>An idea we tried and paused. It isn't deleted, just set aside until the time is right.</dd>
  <dt>Biome</dt><dd>A kind of place in the game, with its own look and its own pieces. The Backyard is the first one.</dd>
  <dt>Blueprint</dt><dd>Unreal Engine's way of building game logic by connecting boxes on a screen, like a flowchart, instead of typing code.</dd>
  <dt>Checkpoint</dt><dd>A saved snapshot of the whole project. If something breaks, we jump back to the last good one.</dd>
  <dt>Frame rate (fps)</dt><dd>How many pictures per second the game draws. 30 looks steady, 60 looks silky smooth. Below about 30, it starts to feel choppy.</dd>
  <dt>Heightmap</dt><dd>A black-and-white picture where brighter means taller. The game turns it into hills and mountains.</dd>
  <dt>HP (health points)</dt><dd>How many hits you can take. Kilo Man has 3.</dd>
  <dt>HUD</dt><dd>The bits drawn on top of the game, like the HP counter and the distance counter.</dd>
  <dt>Mannequin</dt><dd>A plain practice character that comes free with Unreal Engine. Kilo Man borrows one until he gets his own body.</dd>
  <dt>Placeholder</dt><dd>A stand-in, like a temporary sound or model, that does the job until the real one is made.</dd>
  <dt>Play-test</dt><dd>Actually playing the game to see if something works and feels good. Around here, nothing counts as "working" until it's been play-tested.</dd>
  <dt>Rig</dt><dd>The invisible skeleton inside a character that lets it bend and move.</dd>
  <dt>Seed</dt><dd>A number that decides how a level gets laid out. The same seed always makes the same trail.</dd>
  <dt>Segment / trail piece</dt><dd>One chunk of trail, like a straight, a bridge or a vista. The game snaps them together to build a level.</dd>
  <dt>Unreal Engine</dt><dd>The game engine (a big toolkit for making games) that Kilo Man Jar-O is built with. It handles drawing, physics, sound and more.</dd>
  <dt>Vista</dt><dd>An open stretch of trail where you can stop and take in how huge the world is.</dd>
</dl>

*Kilo Man Jar-O is a working title. Everything shown is work in progress.*
