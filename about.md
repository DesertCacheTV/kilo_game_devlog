**Kilo Man Jar-O** (working title) is about a tiny hero named Kilo. He's 10 centimeters tall and weighs about a kilogram, and he runs through a giant garden toward a pickle jar the size of a mountain.

Blades of grass tower like trees. Pebbles are boulders. A barn on the horizon is over a kilometer wide from where he stands.

You run, jump and climb your way along a winding trail, reading what's ahead and picking your route. A run to the jar takes about three minutes, and if you fall off, you're back at the start in under two seconds.

At its heart, it's a game about one simple idea: it doesn't matter how small you are. Big, wonderful things are waiting on the horizon, and you can reach them one step at a time.

The goal is a game that feels calm, cozy and full of wonder, even when the garden gets a little wild.

*Kilo Man Jar-O is a working title. Everything shown is work in progress.*
