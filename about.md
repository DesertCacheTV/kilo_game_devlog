**Kilo** (working title) is about a tiny hero. He's 10 centimeters tall and weighs about a kilogram, and he runs through a giant garden toward a pickle jar the size of a mountain.

Blades of grass tower like trees. Pebbles are boulders. A barn on the horizon is over a kilometer wide from where he stands.

You run, jump and climb your way along a winding trail, reading what's ahead and picking your route. A run to the jar takes about three minutes, and if you fall off, you're back at the start in under two seconds.

The goal is a game that feels calm and full of wonder, even while you're dodging flying pickles.

*Kilo is a working title. Everything shown is work in progress.*
